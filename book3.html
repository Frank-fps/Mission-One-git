Reverent Insanity

Gu Zhen Ren

Rating: 4.7/5

Genre: Xianxia and dark fantasy

Synopsis

Fang Yuan, a Transmigrator from Earth to Gu World, lives through 500 years in the Gu World, travels back in time to the moment he starts his cultivation journey. He is considered an Evil Protagonist that follows the philosophy of the demonic path (A way of life that is akin to pragmatic egoism) with the goal of gaining Eternal Life. The Gu World is a unique world where you cultivate Gu Insects, each with 1 unique ability, to gain power rather than the User themselves having abilities"
